The shared spine

Why one company builds both

Verified Nigerian identity

Every product starts from BVN or NIN verification, so a person is the same person across the group.

Direct integration with local rails

We connect to Nigerian payment rails and licensed institutions directly rather than reselling someone else's connection.

Interfaces that work where people are

WhatsApp and USSD are first-class channels, not fallbacks bolted onto an app.

Confirmation before any consequential action

Money moves and policies issue only after the person has read what will happen and said yes.

Operating principles
P/01

Regulated before launched

A product goes out when the licensing and institutional partnerships behind it are in place, not when the interface is ready. Sorplos Insure distributes cover from NAICOM-licensed underwriters.

P/02

Works on a feature phone

If a service only works on a recent smartphone with good data, it excludes most of the country. WhatsApp and USSD carry the same core actions as the web app.

P/03

Confirmation before consequence

Anything that moves money or binds a policy is restated in plain language and waits for an explicit yes. Nothing consequential is inferred from a conversation.
