Live Banking & payments Venture 01

Eureka

Conversational AI banking on web and WhatsApp. Ask about your money in plain language and act on the answer in the same thread.

Open eureka.sorplos.com What it does

Link your Nigerian bank accounts once, then check balances across all of them, review transactions and see spending and budget insights in one conversation. Nothing sensitive happens without explicit confirmation first.

Capabilities

C/01 — C/06
C/01 Linked bank accounts

Connect Nigerian bank accounts and see balances across all of them in one place.

C/02 Transaction review

Ask what a charge was and read your transactions back in plain language.

C/03 Spending & budget insights

See where money went across accounts and what that means for the month ahead.

C/04 Airtime & data

Buy airtime and data for yourself or someone else from the same thread.

C/05 Bill payments

Pay bills without leaving the conversation or re-entering your details.

C/06 Transfer requests, confirmed

Request a transfer and read back exactly what will happen before you approve it.
